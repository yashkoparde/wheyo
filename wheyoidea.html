Phase 01 — The Friction Bottleneck

Why fitness apps fail college athletes

Most fitness apps demand tedious manual input, counting raw grams of chicken, or staring at abstract charts that give zero immediate reward. People quit within 4 days because the feedback loop is too long.

Simple Truth: If tracking takes work and offers no immediate reward, people abandon it.

1-Tap Workout Presets

Wheyo removes input pain. Pick preset routines (*Push Day, Pull Day, 5K Shred*) or search from 150+ exercises. Hit save, and your volume lifted and calories burned compute deterministically in real time.

Workout logged ➔ Instant Calories & Tonnage calculated
